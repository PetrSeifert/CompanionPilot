---
id: web-research
title: Web Research
description: Gather and validate external factual information, especially recency-sensitive facts.
tags: [research, web, facts, recency]
---
Use web evidence for unknown factual claims and for recency-sensitive topics such as
news, prices, weather, schedules, regulations, and recommendations.

Prefer concrete queries with clear entities and a time anchor when freshness matters.
Synthesize results conservatively and avoid unsupported claims.
